AnScanner Privacy Policy

AnScanner never uploads your documents to any server. All scanning, processing, and storage happens entirely on your device.

Data Collection

We do not collect, upload, or transmit any of your personal documents, scans, or text recognition results. All file processing occurs 100% offline on your device.

Processing & Storage

Any document scanned with AnScanner is saved only to your device's local storage. You retain complete ownership and control over your data. We have no access to it.

Analytics

We use anonymous crash reporting and basic usage counters to help us improve the app. This data is strictly anonymous and cannot be linked to you. You can disable this at any time in Settings.

Advertising

AnScanner may display ads to support development. This relies only on standard Android Advertising ID (AAID) practices without collecting additional identifiable information.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us at anytesh.dev@gmail.com.
